👋 About Me

Hey, I'm Jacob. I'm a sophomore studying Computer Science at the University of South Florida (class of 2028), based in the Tampa Bay area.

I like building things people actually use. I run TheraWeb Design, a web design and local SEO agency for Tampa Bay small businesses, where every site is hand-coded (no templates, no page builders). On campus I'm the Marketing Lead for Google Developers Group at USF and the Design Lead for SHPE at USF.
